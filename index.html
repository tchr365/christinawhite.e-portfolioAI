ABOUT ME

I’m an educator, coach, and lifelong learner with 17+ years of experience designing learning that helps people grow. Across roles in instruction, curriculum development, and professional development, I’ve built my practice on three principles: understand what learners need, make complex ideas accessible, and give people real opportunities to apply new skills.

I’m currently completing a master’s degree in Curriculum and Instruction with a concentration in Educational Technology, and I’m bringing that foundation into corporate instructional design. I’m drawn to creating engaging, practical learning experiences that help professionals build skills and perform with confidence. My work blends an educator’s depth, a coach’s collaborative mindset, and a steady commitment to improvement.

This portfolio shows how I connect purposeful design, technology, and learner needs to create learning that works.

E-PORTFOLIO

My e-portfolio connects my experience as an educator and instructional coach with my growing expertise in instructional design and educational technology. Through visual design, interactive quizzes and assessments, website development, and educational video production, I explore how digital tools can make learning engaging, accessible, and practical. These projects showcase my work with HTML, CSS, JavaScript, multimedia tools, and generative AI while reflecting my commitment to clear communication and purposeful design. As I complete my master’s degree and pursue a career in corporate instructional design, this portfolio demonstrates my growth in creating learning experiences that help people build skills, apply their knowledge, and perform with confidence.
